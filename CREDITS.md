# Credits and Attribution

## Original ROMRAM research — Dmitry Grinberg

**Dmitry Grinberg** is the original creator and author of **ROMRAM**, the RP2040 external QSPI RAM / memory-mapping experiment that inspired part of this project's research.

Original resources:
- Author's technical write-up: https://dmitry.gr/?proj=10.+RomRam&r=06.+Thoughts
- Original ROMRAM archive: https://dmitry.gr/images/romram.zip
- Independent coverage: https://www.hackster.io/news/dmitry-grinberg-s-romram-adds-8mb-of-external-ram-to-the-humble-raspberry-pi-rp2040-27f2d7363f10

**Credit:** The original ROMRAM concept, circuit approach, bootloader, and fault-handler technique belong to Dmitry Grinberg. The Newport River Hermes Memory Coprocessor is an independent follow-on research experiment exploring Android-compatible memory and compute offloading; it is **not** an official ROMRAM release, endorsed adaptation, or claim of inventing ROMRAM.

The author describes the downloadable ROMRAM source as BSD 2-Clause licensed. We have **not yet imported or inspected the archive**. If any original source is imported, preserve its exact copyright notices, BSD-2-Clause license text, and provenance. Do not assume hardware schematics or other assets are licensed identically without checking.
